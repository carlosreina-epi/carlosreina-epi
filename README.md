# Carlos Alberto Reina Bolaños, PhD

Epidemiologist working on vaccine effectiveness and safety, and on causal inference with large linked health data. PhD in Epidemiology (Universidad de Antioquia, 2024), MPH (Universidad del Valle). Currently a Clinical Data Analyst at Atropos Health, where I design and run comparative effectiveness and safety analyses on electronic health records under the target trial framework.

Before that I coordinated population surveillance and demographic analysis for Cali during the COVID-19 pandemic, led a real-world effectiveness study of CoronaVac in four Colombian cities with COVEP and China CDC, and appraised evidence for national health technology assessment at IETS.

## What I publish here

**[covid19-vaccine-effectiveness-colombia](https://github.com/carlosreina-epi/covid19-vaccine-effectiveness-colombia)** — A target trial emulation of Colombia's National Vaccination Plan, rebuilt from my doctoral work as a fully reproducible pipeline. Includes a synthetic data generator with a known true effect, risk-set matching, and a comparison showing how much the estimate depends on how time zero and the comparison group are defined. [Read the rendered report](https://carlosreina-epi.github.io/covid19-vaccine-effectiveness-colombia/)

## Research interests

Vaccine effectiveness and safety, including real-world evidence from national registries and post-licensure surveillance. Causal inference on healthcare databases: target trial emulation, propensity score methods, survival analysis, and the failure modes that arise when administrative data are treated as if they were trial data. Population and environmental health in middle-income settings.

## Tools

R (advanced: survival, MatchIt, coxme, tidyverse, gtsummary), SQL and BigQuery, Python (intermediate), Power BI, Quarto. I care more about analyses that can be re-run than about the tools used to produce them.
